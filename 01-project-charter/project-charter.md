# Project Charter

The Product Charter defines the vision, purpose, target users, business context, and key objectives of the language-learning platform. The product is designed for individuals and organizations seeking to develop professional language skills, with a particular focus on industry-specific communication in fields such as medicine, law, and other specialized professions.

The platform aims to make professional language learning more effective, structured, and accessible by supporting the way the human brain naturally processes, retains, and recalls information. It is designed to facilitate continuous learning, improve professional communication, and help users independently monitor and record their learning progress.

The Product Charter establishes the overall direction of the product and serves as a reference point for defining its features, priorities, user needs, and future development.

## Project Name

Online Language Platform Launch

## Project Role

Project Coordinator

## Project Type

Simulated Project of Language Project Management for medical and law professions.

## Project Methodology

Agile / Scrum

## Project Duration

6 weeks

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to plan and coordinate the launch
of an online language learning platform that enables students or businesses to:

- create an account
- find a tutor
- view tutor availability
- book lessons for individual or group
- access learning materials
- receive booking confirmations
- track progress 
- provide feedback after lessons

## Business Goal

Create a simple and scalable online learning experience for a specific group 
of professionals while reducing manual administration for tutors and support staff.

## Key Stakeholders

- Project Manager
- Product Owner
- Students
- Tutors
- Customer Support
- Product / IT Team
- Management

## Success Criteria

- Platform launched within 6 weeks
- 20 tutors successfully onboarded
- Students can book lessons online
- Lesson confirmation process is automated
- Learning materials are accessible online
- Customer satisfaction target: 4.5/5
- The income from the project exceeds the costs of its arrangement by at least 50%
