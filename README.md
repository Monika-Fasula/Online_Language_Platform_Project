# Online Language Platform Launch

A project is about creating a learning management system for tutors and students who are interested in online learning for professional environment. Language learning management system concentrates on medical language and law communication profficiency. 

Provides lessons and organize schedules and content for 12 languages: Arabic, English, Chinese, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hungarian, Italian, Polish, Portugal, Spanish. 

Project is based on gamification and takes into account cognitive overload. Lessons are organized in intervals of sessions of pomodoro technique - 25 minutes for persistent study and 5 minutes for a break. Students have access to the materials from previous, finished lesson also off-line.

The learning management system is skill-based platform with guiding and keeping track of effectiveness of learning. Teachers or tutors can observe in what range is the material aquisition and what skills need particular attention.

## User Role

User Roles

The platform provides different user roles, each with a specific set of responsibilities and permissions. Access to system features and data is determined by the assigned role.

### Student

Students use the platform to participate in courses and manage their learning activities. They can access assigned learning materials, communicate with teachers, attend consultations, manage their notes, and receive notifications.

Typical permissions include:

Viewing and downloading assigned learning materials
Searching for available materials
Adding and managing personal notes
Communicating with teachers
Requesting and managing consultations
Viewing schedules and notifications
Creating and tracking support tickets
Managing their personal profile

### Teacher

Teachers are responsible for delivering courses, managing educational content, and communicating with students and parents.

Typical permissions include:

Managing assigned courses
Uploading and managing learning materials
Communicating with students
Providing feedback and educational information
Managing consultations and availability
Viewing relevant student information
Managing course-related schedules
Receiving system notifications


### Administrator

Administrators are responsible for managing the platform and its users. They have broader permissions than regular users and can access administrative and business-management functions.

Typical permissions include:

Managing user accounts and roles
Managing students and teachers
Managing courses and learning materials
Managing schedules
Managing payments
Generating reports
Managing access permissions
Monitoring platform activity
Configuring system-level settings

### IT / Support

IT and support staff are responsible for handling technical issues and assisting users with problems related to the platform.

Typical permissions include:

Receiving and managing support tickets
Communicating with users regarding technical issues
Updating ticket status and information
Investigating reported technical problems
Resolving and closing support tickets
Accessing technical information required for troubleshooting
Role-Based Access Control

The platform uses role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure that users can only access features and information appropriate to their role. Permissions should follow the principle of least privilege, meaning that users receive only the access necessary to perform their responsibilities.

# How to Use It

The LMS platform provides different features depending on the account type. The available account types are **Student**, **Tutor/Teacher**, **Business**, **Platform Manager**, and **Admin**.

## 1. Account & Access Management

### Login

Users can log in using their name, surname, email address, and account type.

### Registration

To create an account, enter your name, surname, email address, phone number, and select the appropriate account type.

### Password Reset

If you forget or want to change your password, click the **Reset** button at the bottom of the website and enter the email address used during registration.

### Account Verification

User authentication is protected by two-factor authentication. A verification code is sent to the phone number associated with the account.

If you no longer have access to your registered phone number, contact IT Support.

### Profile Management

Users can:

* edit their personal information;
* manage accessibility settings;
* upload personal documents to their designated folder;
* view their participation and absence dates.

### Account Deletion

To permanently delete an account, go to **My Account â†’ Data Management** and select the account deletion option.

The system will send an SMS confirmation code to verify the account deletion request.

## 2. Learning Setup & Onboarding

### Student

#### Choose a Target Language

Go to **My Profile â†’ Languages**.

The **Languages** section displays the available languages. Select **Add** to add a language to your profile.

#### Change Target Language

Go to **My Languages** and select **Change Language**.

The system will ask you to confirm the change.

If you withdraw from a course using the **Drop Out** option in your student profile, the language selection can also be updated automatically. The system will notify you about the change.

#### Determine Language Level

In **My Languages**, select **Level**.

Choose your current language level from **A1 to C2**. After selecting a level, the system will display a confirmation.

#### Learning Goals

The **Progress** section displays your learning progress, including completed lessons, homework, tests, and project assessments.

You can also add your own learning goals, expectations, or topics that you would like to discuss during future lessons.

#### Learning Preferences

You can adjust your learning preferences, including:

* target language;
* language level;
* course intensity;
* lesson schedule.

Go to **Progress â†’ Schedule** to manage your schedule.

Depending on the applicable scheduling rules, you can cancel, book, or reschedule lessons from the calendar. Schedule changes can be made up to 24 hours before a lesson.

#### Initial Assessment

Go to **My Profile â†’ Assessment** to access the initial language assessment.

The assessment contains multiple-choice questions designed to evaluate grammar, vocabulary, speaking, and writing skills.

The assessment is time-limited. Incorrect answers may result in negative points according to the assessment scoring rules.

After completing the assessment, the system evaluates your language proficiency and recommends an appropriate language level.

---

### Tutor/Teacher

#### Choose a Teaching Language

Go to **My Profile â†’ Languages**.

The **Languages** section displays the available languages. Select **Sign Up** to add a language you teach.

#### Change Teaching Language

Go to **My Profile â†’ Languages** and select **Add** to add another available language.

#### Determine Language Level

In **My Languages**, select **Level** and choose the appropriate level from **A1 to C2**.

The system will display a confirmation after the level has been selected.

#### Schedule Availability

Tutors/Teachers can add their available dates and hours to the calendar.

The schedule can be modified up to 48 hours before a lesson. Tutors/Teachers can change their schedule up to two times per week.

Schedule changes are subject to management approval. If the available number of changes has been exceeded, the system will notify the Tutor/Teacher that further changes are not available.

#### Choosing Groups

In **My Profile â†’ Teaching**, Tutors/Teachers can select up to three groups per month.

Select a group from the available list to add it to the teaching schedule.

#### Contract Preview

Tutors/Teachers can view their signed company contracts in **Documents**.

Access to a contract requires password and code confirmation. Contracts can be downloaded.

If contract information is incorrect or expired, select **Change Data** to submit a request to update the information.

#### Student Assessment and Management

In **Teaching**, Tutors/Teachers can access the assessment area.

They can search for a specific group or student and:

* plan tests and exams;
* edit lesson topics;
* record attendance;
* adjust the difficulty of learning materials;
* view student progress;
* send notifications to students or groups;
* view relevant contact details.

Tutors/Teachers receive a notification when a student's progress deteriorates.

### Business

#### Choose a Target Language

Go to **My Profile â†’ Languages**.

#### Change Target Language

## 3. Lesson & Scheduling Management

### Book a Lesson

Lessons can be booked through the main call-to-action button, through **Courses** or **Fees**, or directly from a selected course after logging into the user profile.

Users can book multiple lessons. Participation in a lesson requires successful payment.

If payment has been completed but access to the booked lesson is still unavailable, contact IT Support.

### Reschedule a Lesson

Students and Business users can reschedule lessons with at least 48 hours' notice.

Go to **Progress â†’ Schedule** and select the lesson that you want to reschedule.

### Cancel a Lesson

Students and Business users can cancel lessons with at least 48 hours' notice, according to the applicable cancellation policy.

Go to **Progress â†’ Schedule** and select the lesson that you want to cancel.

### Define Lesson Scope

The lesson scope can be modified by the Tutor/Teacher, Student, or Business user with at least 48 hours' notice.

**Tutor/Teacher:**
Go to **Teaching â†’ Scope** to modify the lesson scope.

**Student:**
Submit a lesson scope modification request to the Tutor/Teacher from the **Progress** section. The request can be submitted through the contact form available on the page.

**Business:**
Go to **Participants**, select an individual participant or group, and use the **Modify** option to submit a lesson scope proposal.

### Tutor/Teacher Availability

Tutor/Teacher availability is determined by contractual conditions and personal preferences.

Students and Business users can send an availability request for a specific date and time. The Tutor/Teacher can accept or reject the request.

### Student Availability

Students can provide information about their availability through the Tutor/Teacher contact form or by updating their schedule.

Holiday periods can be added through **Progress â†’ Schedule**.

### Business Scheduling

Business users can provide availability information for individual participants or groups through the schedule or by contacting the Tutor/Teacher.

Holiday periods can also be added through the schedule.

## 5. Learning & Assessment

### Lessons

Lessons are organized according to the student's skills, knowledge, and learning level.

Students can change their Tutor/Teacher up to two times per year.

If a lesson cannot be organized by the assigned Tutor/Teacher, the Student or Business user will receive a notification.

A 60-minute individual lesson consists of two sessions with one 5-minute break.

A 90-minute individual lesson consists of three sessions with two breaks: one 5-minute break and one 10-minute break.

### Tests

Tutors/Teachers organize tests after approximately **30% and 60%** of the course material has been completed.

Test results are available in **Progress â†’ Tests**.

### Exams

Tutors/Teachers organize exams after **100% of the course material** has been presented.

Students receive an email notification about the exam.

The notification provides access to the **Exams** section. Students must authenticate using their password and the verification code received by email.

Exam results are available in **Progress â†’ Exams**.

Students can take the exam up to two times during the course.

### Project Tasks

Students can select projects from **Progress â†’ Projects**.

Each student is required to complete five projects during the course.

Project assessments are displayed in **Progress â†’ Projects**.

### Vocabulary Repetition

Tutors/Teachers can assign vocabulary and glossary revision before the next lesson.

Students can access assigned flashcards from **Progress** through the notification displayed above the calendar.

Each term is displayed multiple times to support repetition and memorization.

### Progress Tracking

To view progress, log in and go to **My Profile â†’ Progress**.

The progress page displays the percentage of completed course material.

Students can also view their performance in individual language skills.

### AI Copilot

AI Copilot sessions are designed to support the assessment of a student's learning progress.

During a conversation with the student, AI Copilot identifies:

* strengths;
* weaknesses;
* areas that require additional practice;
* material that should be reviewed.

AI Copilot is not intended to complete homework or replace the Tutor/Teacher. Its purpose is to support the diagnosis and monitoring of the student's learning progress.

### Course Completion

When the student reaches **100% progress** and completes the required examinations, the course completion status is displayed in the **Progress** section.

The system sends a notification confirming the course completion status.
